About VitalSign

Healthcare Readmission Prediction System

What is VitalSign?


VitalSign is a machine learning-based healthcare prediction system designed to analyze diabetic patient information and predict hospital readmission risk.


The system uses patient encounter information, medication details, laboratory procedures, diagnoses and other healthcare attributes.


The primary machine learning target is the patient's readmission status.
