About VitalSign

AI-Driven Clinical Healthcare Analytics & 30-Day Readmission Risk Stratification

Platform Mission

VitalSign is a full-stack healthcare machine learning application developed to assess the likelihood of diabetic patient readmissions within 30 days of hospital discharge. Unplanned hospital readmissions impose significant clinical burdens on patient outcomes and substantial costs on healthcare institutions. VitalSign utilizes historical multi-hospital clinical encounter data to provide automated, explainable risk assessments.

📊 The Dataset

Built on the renowned UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals dataset (1999–2008), comprising 50,000 patient records across 130 medical centers. Key dimensions include:

  • Demographics: Age, Gender, Race
  • Encounter metrics: Time in hospital, laboratory procedures, medication counts
  • Inpatient / Outpatient / Emergency utilization history
  • Primary, secondary, and tertiary ICD-9 diagnoses
  • 23 diabetes medications including Insulin titration and dosage changes

⚙️ Machine Learning Pipeline

A production-ready data science pipeline incorporating:

  • Median imputation for clinical numeric indicators
  • Most-frequent imputation and One-Hot Encoding for categorical features
  • Exclusion of direct identifiers and target leakage variables
  • Stratified train-test partitioning (80/20) preserving class proportions
  • Benchmarked against Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, and Random Forests

Clinical Risk Stratification Framework

Low Risk

Predicted probability < 0.30 (< 30%). Routine outpatient follow-up recommended.

Moderate Risk

Predicted probability between 0.30 and 0.59. Closer post-discharge medication review indicated.

High Risk

Predicted probability ≥ 0.60 (≥ 60%). Intensive care management and transitional support advised.

This application is an educational machine learning prediction system and is not a medical diagnosis tool. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
