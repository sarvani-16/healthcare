Why F1-Score Selection?

The UCI Diabetes dataset exhibits substantial class imbalance (only 11.49% readmitted < 30 days). A naive model predicting 0 would achieve 88.5% accuracy but zero clinical utility. By prioritizing the F1-score, we balance sensitivity (detecting at-risk patients) and precision (preventing alert fatigue).
