VitalSign

Healthcare Readmission Prediction System

VitalSign is an educational machine learning platform that analyzes the UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals dataset (50,000 clinical records) to predict whether a diabetic patient is likely to be readmitted within 30 days of discharge.
