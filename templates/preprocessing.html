Pipeline Architecture Steps

1
Data Ingestion & Missing Symbol Normalization

Loads 50,000 encounters with 50 attributes. Detects and substitutes UCI Diabetes missing value placeholder character '?' with standard NumPy NaN.

2
Target Formulation (Readmission_30_Days)

Maps original multiclass readmitted (<30, >30, NO) into binary target: 1 for readmissions occurring within 30 days of discharge; 0 otherwise.

3
Removal of Identifiers & Target Leakage Columns

Drops encounter_id, patient_nbr (uninformative arbitrary keys), weight (96.3% missing), payer_code (65.3% missing administrative code), medical_specialty (35.5% missing/high cardinality), and the original readmitted column to prevent leakage.

4
Automated Numerical & Categorical Pipeline (ColumnTransformer)

Numerical features are imputed with SimpleImputer(strategy='median'). Categorical features are imputed with SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent') and encoded via OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore').

5
Stratified Train-Test Partitioning

Uses train_test_split(test_size=0.2, random_state=42, stratify=y) ensuring 40,000 training records and 10,000 testing records maintain exact 11.49% class proportion.

🔢 11 Numerical Features (Median Imputed)

  • admission_type_id (Emergency, Urgent, Elective)
  • discharge_disposition_id (Home, SNF, Rehab)
  • admission_source_id (Physician Referral, ER)
  • time_in_hospital (1 to 14 days)
  • num_lab_procedures (Total laboratory tests)
  • num_procedures (Non-lab interventions)
  • num_medications (Distinct active medications)
  • number_outpatient (Outpatient visits preceding encounter)
  • number_emergency (Emergency visits preceding encounter)
  • number_inpatient (Inpatient admissions preceding encounter)
  • number_diagnoses (Recorded clinical diagnoses)

🔤 33 Categorical Features (One-Hot Encoded)

Imputed with most frequent class and transformed into binary dummy vectors:

  • Demographics: race, gender, age
  • ICD-9 Diagnoses: diag_1, diag_2, diag_3
  • Laboratory Results: max_glu_serum, A1Cresult
  • Medications: metformin, repaglinide, nateglinide, chlorpropamide, glimepiride, acetohexamide, glipizide, glyburide, tolbutamide, pioglitazone, rosiglitazone, acarbose, miglitol, troglitazone, tolazamide, examide, citoglipton, insulin, glyburide_metformin, glipizide_metformin, glimepiride_pioglitazone, metformin_rosiglitazone, metformin_pioglitazone
  • Regimen Change & Status: change, diabetesMed
